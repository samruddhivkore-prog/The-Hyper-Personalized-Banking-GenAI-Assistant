# Overdraft Protection

Overdraft Protection helps an Aisling Bank customer avoid a declined payment when their current account balance would otherwise go negative. When enabled, it automatically transfers funds from a linked savings account to cover a transaction that would overdraw the current account; each such transfer costs €5.00. If no linked account has enough funds and Aisling Bank still covers the transaction under its standard overdraft policy, a €30.00 overdraft fee applies per transaction, up to a maximum of 4 fees per day, or €120.00. Customers have until 9:00 PM local time on the day of the overdraft to bring the account positive and avoid the fee. Overdraft Protection is available to any customer with an active current account and at least one other eligible linked account; it is opt-in and can be switched on or off at any time in the mobile app. Without it, a transaction that would overdraw the account is simply declined, free of charge.
