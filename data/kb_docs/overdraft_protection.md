# Overdraft Protection

Overdraft Protection helps prevent declined transactions when a checking account
balance would otherwise go negative.

## How it works
When enabled, Overdraft Protection automatically transfers funds from a linked
savings account to cover a transaction that would overdraw the checking account. If
no linked account has sufficient funds, the bank may still cover the transaction at
its discretion under the standard overdraft policy.

## Fees
Each transfer from a linked savings account to cover an overdraft costs $5.00. If no
linked account is available and the bank covers the transaction under standard
overdraft policy, a $30.00 overdraft fee applies per transaction, up to a maximum of
4 fees per day ($120.00).

## Grace period
Customers have until 9:00 PM local time on the day of the overdraft to deposit funds
and bring the account positive before the overdraft fee is charged.

## Eligibility
Overdraft Protection is available to any customer with an active checking account and
at least one other eligible linked account (savings, or a credit line where offered).
It is opt-in and must be enabled through the mobile app or online banking.

## Opting out
Customers can disable Overdraft Protection at any time. Without it, transactions that
would overdraw the account are simply declined at no cost, rather than covered for a
fee.
