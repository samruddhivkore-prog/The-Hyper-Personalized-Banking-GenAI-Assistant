# SEPA Credit Transfer

A SEPA Credit Transfer is Aisling Bank's standard way to send euro payments to any bank account in Ireland or the wider Single Euro Payments Area. A standard SEPA Credit Transfer is free of charge for personal current accounts and settles by the end of the next business day. Personal accounts may send up to €10,000 per day and €25,000 per rolling 7-day period via a standard SEPA Credit Transfer. If a transfer is returned, for example because of an incorrect IBAN, the funds are automatically returned to the sender's account within 1-2 business days at no charge. Customers who need the money to arrive within seconds rather than the next business day should use Real-Time Payments, which runs on the SEPA Instant Credit Transfer scheme for a €1.00 fee. Customers sending more than €5,000 outside the SEPA area should use a wire transfer instead.

How to send a SEPA Credit Transfer in the app.
1. Open the Aisling Bank app and tap Send Money.
2. Enter the recipient's IBAN and the amount.
3. Choose Standard Transfer and confirm.
4. The funds leave your account and arrive by the next business day.
