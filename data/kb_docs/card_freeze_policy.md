# Card Freeze and Lost Card Policy

A customer can freeze an Aisling Bank debit or credit card instantly in the mobile app. Freezing is free and fully reversible: a frozen card blocks new purchases and withdrawals immediately, but can be unfrozen just as instantly if the card is found or no longer at risk. Freezing a card does not close the account and does not affect any pending or already-cleared transactions. If a card is lost or stolen, the customer should freeze it immediately in the app, then contact Aisling Bank to report it lost or stolen so a replacement can be issued; a replacement card arrives within 5-7 business days, or 1-2 business days for expedited shipping for a €15 fee.

How to freeze a card in the app.
1. Open the Aisling Bank app and go to Cards.
2. Select the card you want to freeze.
3. Tap Freeze Card and confirm.
4. The card is blocked immediately; you can unfreeze it the same way at any time.
