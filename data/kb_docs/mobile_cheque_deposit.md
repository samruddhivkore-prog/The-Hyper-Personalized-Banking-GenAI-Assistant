# Mobile Cheque Deposit

Mobile Cheque Deposit lets Aisling Bank customers deposit a paper cheque by taking a photo in the mobile app, without visiting a branch or ATM. Customers can deposit up to €5,000 per cheque and up to €10,000 in total per rolling 30-day period; larger amounts must be deposited at a branch. The first €225 of a mobile cheque deposit is typically available the next business day, with the remaining balance available within 2 business days; holds of up to 7 business days may apply for large deposits, new accounts, or deposits flagged for review. A cheque must be endorsed on the back with the account holder's signature and the words "For Mobile Deposit Only" to be accepted. Mobile Cheque Deposit is available to any customer with an active current or savings account and the mobile app installed; there is no minimum account tenure required.

How to deposit a cheque in the app.
1. Open the Aisling Bank app and tap Deposit Cheque.
2. Endorse the back of the cheque with your signature and "For Mobile Deposit Only".
3. Photograph the front and back of the cheque as prompted.
4. Confirm the amount and submit; the first €225 is available the next business day.
