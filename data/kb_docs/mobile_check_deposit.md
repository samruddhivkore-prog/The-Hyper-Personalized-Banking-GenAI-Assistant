# Mobile Check Deposit

Mobile Check Deposit lets customers deposit paper checks by taking a photo through
the mobile app, without visiting a branch or ATM.

## Deposit limits
Customers can deposit up to $5,000 per check and up to $10,000 total per rolling
30-day period using Mobile Check Deposit. Customers who need to deposit larger
amounts should visit a branch or use an ATM.

## Funds availability
The first $225 of a mobile deposit is typically available the next business day.
The remaining balance is generally available within 2 business days, though holds of
up to 7 business days may apply for large deposits, new accounts, or deposits flagged
for review.

## How to endorse the check
Checks must be endorsed on the back with the account holder's signature and the
words "For Mobile Deposit Only" to be accepted. Checks without this endorsement may
be rejected or delayed for manual review.

## Eligibility
Mobile Check Deposit is available to any customer with an active checking or savings
account and the mobile app installed. There is no minimum account tenure required.

## Common rejection reasons
Deposits are commonly rejected for blurry images, stale-dated checks older than 6
months, missing endorsements, or checks that have already been deposited elsewhere.
