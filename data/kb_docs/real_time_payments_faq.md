# Real-Time Payments FAQ

Real-Time Payments (RTP) lets you send money to another bank account and have it
arrive in seconds, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and holidays.

## How much does it cost?
Each Real-Time Payment costs a flat fee of $1.00 per transfer, deducted from the
sending account at the time of the transfer. There is no fee to receive a Real-Time
Payment.

## What is the transfer limit?
You can send up to $2,500 per transaction and up to $5,000 per rolling 24-hour period
using Real-Time Payments. Limits reset automatically and are not adjustable through
customer support without a manual review.

## How fast does it actually arrive?
Funds are available in the recipient's account within seconds of the transfer being
confirmed. This is different from ACH transfers, which settle in 1-3 business days,
and wire transfers, which settle same-day but only during business hours.

## Which accounts support Real-Time Payments?
Real-Time Payments are available for checking accounts in good standing that have
been open for at least 30 days. Savings accounts and business accounts are not yet
eligible for outbound Real-Time Payments.

## Can I cancel a Real-Time Payment?
No. Because funds settle within seconds, Real-Time Payments cannot be canceled or
reversed once submitted. Double-check the recipient's account details before
confirming.
