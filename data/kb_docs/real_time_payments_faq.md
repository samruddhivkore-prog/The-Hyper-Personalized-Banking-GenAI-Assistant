# Real-Time Payments

Aisling Bank's Real-Time Payments service sends money to another bank account using the SEPA Instant Credit Transfer scheme. Funds arrive within seconds, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and bank holidays. Each Real-Time Payment costs a flat fee of €1.00 per transfer. There is no fee to receive a Real-Time Payment. You can send up to €2,500 per transaction and up to €5,000 per rolling 24-hour period using Real-Time Payments. Real-Time Payments are available to personal current accounts that have been open for at least 30 days. Because funds settle within seconds, a Real-Time Payment cannot be cancelled or reversed once submitted, so customers should confirm the recipient's IBAN carefully before submitting. For transfers that are not time-sensitive, a standard SEPA Credit Transfer is free and settles by the next business day.

How to send a Real-Time Payment in the app.
1. Open the Aisling Bank app and tap Send Money.
2. Enter the recipient's IBAN and the amount.
3. Choose Real-Time Payment and confirm.
4. The funds arrive in the recipient's account within seconds.
