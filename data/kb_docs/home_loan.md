# Home Loan

An Aisling Bank Home Loan is a mortgage for buying or building a home in Ireland; it cannot be used for any other purpose. First-time buyers need a minimum deposit of 10% of the property value, and second-time or second-home buyers need a minimum deposit of 20%. Home Loan amounts range from €50,000 to €1,000,000, with terms of up to 35 years. A fixed rate of 3.95% to 4.75% is available for terms of 1 to 10 years, after which the rate reverts to the variable rate then in force; a variable rate of 4.15% to 4.95% is available for the full term. There is no application fee and no early repayment penalty on the variable rate; a fixed-rate loan repaid early may incur a breakage fee based on prevailing interest rates at the time. To be eligible, an applicant must be at least 18 years old, have proof of income and a deposit, and pass a credit and affordability assessment; a second home purchase also requires the applicant's existing mortgage, if any, to be in good standing. A mortgage decision typically takes 2 to 3 weeks from a complete application, and approved funds are released at the property's closing date.

How to apply for a Home Loan in the app.
1. Open the Aisling Bank app and tap Apply for a Home Loan.
2. Enter the property value and your deposit amount.
3. Upload proof of income and identification as prompted.
4. Submit your application; a mortgage advisor will contact you within 2 business days.
