# Channel Preference Policy

Customers can choose how they prefer to interact with the bank: mobile app, online
banking (web), phone, or in-branch. This preference influences which products and
communications are surfaced first.

## Setting a channel preference
Customers can set or change their preferred channel at any time in the mobile app
under Settings > Communication Preferences, or by asking a branch representative to
update it on their behalf.

## Digital-first customers
Customers who primarily use mobile or web channels are automatically enrolled in
paperless statements and are prioritized for digital-only product launches, such as
early access to new mobile app features.

## Branch-oriented customers
Customers who prefer phone or in-branch service continue to receive paper statements
by default and are prioritized for in-branch appointment scheduling reminders. They
can switch to digital channels at any time without losing any account features.

## Does channel preference affect fees or rates?
No. Channel preference does not affect interest rates, fees, or loan terms on any
product. It only affects how the bank communicates with the customer and which
features are surfaced first in marketing and recommendations.

## Multi-channel access
Regardless of preference, all customers retain full access to every channel (app,
web, phone, branch) at all times. Setting a preference only changes default behavior,
not access.
