# Channel Preference

An Aisling Bank customer can choose how they prefer to deal with the bank: mobile app, online banking, phone, or in branch, and this preference affects which products and messages are shown first. A customer can change their preferred channel at any time under Communication Preferences in the mobile app, or by asking a branch representative to update it. A customer who mainly uses mobile or online banking is automatically moved to paperless statements and is shown new digital features first. A customer who prefers phone or branch service keeps paper statements by default and is prioritised for branch appointment reminders, and can switch to digital channels at any time without losing any account feature. Channel preference never affects interest rates, fees, or loan terms on any product; every customer keeps full access to every channel regardless of their stated preference.
