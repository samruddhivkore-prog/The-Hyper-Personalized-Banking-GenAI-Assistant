# Wire Transfer Fees

A wire transfer is a same-day, high-value transfer method typically used for large
payments such as real estate closings or business-to-business payments.

## Domestic wire fees
An outgoing domestic wire transfer costs $25.00 per transfer. An incoming domestic
wire transfer costs $10.00. There is no minimum transfer amount, but wires are
generally recommended only for transfers above $5,000, since Real-Time Payments and
ACH transfers are cheaper for smaller amounts.

## International wire fees
An outgoing international wire transfer costs $45.00 per transfer, plus any fees
charged by intermediary banks, which the sending bank does not control. Incoming
international wires cost $15.00.

## How fast do wires arrive?
Domestic wires submitted before 3:00 PM local time on a business day are typically
delivered the same business day. Wires submitted after the cutoff, or on a weekend
or holiday, are processed the next business day. International wires typically take
1-2 business days depending on the destination country and intermediary banks.

## Can a wire be reversed?
Wire transfers cannot be reversed or canceled once they have been released for
processing. Contact support immediately if you believe a wire was sent in error;
recovery is not guaranteed and depends on the receiving bank's cooperation.

## Wire transfer limits
Personal accounts may send up to $100,000 per wire without additional manual review.
Transfers above that amount require identity verification and may take an additional
business day to process.
