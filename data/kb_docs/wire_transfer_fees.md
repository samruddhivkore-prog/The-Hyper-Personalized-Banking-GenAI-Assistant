# Wire Transfer Fees

A wire transfer is Aisling Bank's same-day, high-value transfer method, typically used for large payments such as property purchases or business-to-business payments outside the SEPA area. An outgoing domestic wire transfer costs €25.00 per transfer. An outgoing international wire transfer costs €45.00 per transfer, plus any fee charged by intermediary banks, which Aisling Bank does not control. An incoming wire transfer, domestic or international, costs €10.00. Domestic wires submitted before 3:00 PM local time on a business day are typically delivered the same business day. International wires typically take 1-2 business days depending on the destination country. Personal accounts may send up to €100,000 per wire without additional manual review; larger amounts require identity verification and may take an extra business day. A wire transfer cannot be reversed once it has been released for processing.
