# High-Yield Savings

The Aisling Bank High-Yield Savings account is for customers who want to grow a steady balance without actively investing it. The account currently earns 3.50% Annual Equivalent Rate, variable and subject to change, with interest calculated daily and credited monthly. There is no minimum balance to open the account and no monthly fee, though a balance that stays below €100 for more than 60 consecutive days may be converted to a standard savings account. Customers may make up to 9 withdrawals or transfers out per statement cycle before a €10 fee applies per additional withdrawal. The account is available to any customer whose current account has been open for at least 6 months, and a customer may hold only one High-Yield Savings account at a time. Existing customers can open one instantly in the mobile app, with funds transferable in immediately from a current account.
