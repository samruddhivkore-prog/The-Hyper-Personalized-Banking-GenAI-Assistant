# High-Yield Savings Account

The High-Yield Savings account is designed for customers who want to grow a stable
balance without actively trading or investing.

## Interest rate
The account currently earns 4.25% Annual Percentage Yield (APY), variable and subject
to change with market conditions. Interest is compounded daily and credited monthly.

## Minimum balance and fees
There is no minimum balance required to open the account, and no monthly maintenance
fee. However, balances that stay below $100 for more than 60 consecutive days may be
automatically converted to a standard savings account.

## Withdrawal limits
Federal regulations historically limited savings withdrawals to 6 per month; this
account currently allows up to 9 withdrawals or transfers out per statement cycle
before a $10 excess-withdrawal fee applies per additional transaction.

## Eligibility
Available to any customer with an account open for at least 6 months in good
standing. Customers who already hold a High-Yield Savings account are not eligible to
open a second one.

## How to open
Existing customers can open a High-Yield Savings account instantly through the mobile
app or online banking, with no additional paperwork required, and funds can be moved
in immediately from an existing checking account.
