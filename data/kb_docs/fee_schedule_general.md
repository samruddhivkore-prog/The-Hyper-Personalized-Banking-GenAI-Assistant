# General Account Fees

This is a summary of common Aisling Bank account fees not covered in a product-specific policy. A standard current account has no monthly fee when it receives at least one salary or income deposit per month, or keeps a minimum daily balance of €500; otherwise a €12.00 monthly fee applies. There is no fee to use an Aisling Bank ATM; an out-of-network ATM withdrawal costs €2.50 from Aisling Bank, in addition to any fee the ATM operator charges. Customers who keep paper statements pay €3.00 per month, waived automatically by switching to electronic statements in the mobile app. A stop-payment request on a payment costs €30.00, whether or not the payment is successfully stopped. Closing an account is free once it has been open for at least 90 days; closing within 90 days of opening incurs a €25.00 early-closure fee. If a deposited cheque is returned unpaid by the issuing bank, a €12.00 returned-item fee applies.
