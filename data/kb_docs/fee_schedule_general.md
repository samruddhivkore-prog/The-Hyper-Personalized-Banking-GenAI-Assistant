# General Fee Schedule

This document summarizes common account fees not covered in product-specific policy
documents.

## Monthly maintenance fees
Standard checking accounts carry a $0 monthly maintenance fee when the account
receives at least one direct deposit per month or maintains a minimum daily balance
of $500. Otherwise, a $12.00 monthly maintenance fee applies.

## ATM fees
There is no fee for using an ATM within the bank's network. Out-of-network ATM
withdrawals incur a $2.50 fee from the bank, in addition to any fee charged by the
ATM operator.

## Paper statement fee
Customers enrolled in paper statements are charged $3.00 per month. This fee is
waived for customers who switch to electronic statements through the mobile app or
online banking.

## Stop payment fee
Requesting a stop payment on a check or recurring payment costs $30.00 per request,
regardless of whether the payment is ultimately stopped successfully.

## Account closure
There is no fee to close an account, provided the account has been open for at least
90 days. Accounts closed within 90 days of opening may incur a $25.00 early closure
fee.

## Returned deposit fee
If a deposited check is returned unpaid by the issuing bank, a $12.00 returned
deposit fee applies to the depositor's account.
