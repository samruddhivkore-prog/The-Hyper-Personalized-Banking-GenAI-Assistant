# Security and Fraud Policy

This document describes how the bank protects customer accounts and what to do if
fraud is suspected.

## Zero liability for unauthorized transactions
Customers are not held liable for unauthorized transactions reported within 60 days
of the statement date on which the transaction first appeared, provided the customer
did not share their login credentials or PIN with the person responsible.

## Reporting fraud
Suspected fraud should be reported immediately through the mobile app's "Report a
Problem" flow or by calling the number on the back of the debit or credit card. Cards
reported as compromised are deactivated within minutes and a replacement is issued
within 5-7 business days, or 1-2 business days for expedited shipping for a $15 fee.

## Multi-factor authentication
Login attempts from a new device require a one-time passcode sent via SMS or email in
addition to the account password. Customers can also enable biometric login
(fingerprint or face recognition) in the mobile app.

## Transaction alerts
Customers can enable real-time push notifications, SMS, or email alerts for any
transaction above a threshold they set (as low as $1.00). These alerts are free and
configurable in the mobile app under Security Settings.

## Account lockout
After 5 consecutive failed login attempts, the account is temporarily locked for 30
minutes. Customers can unlock immediately by verifying identity through a one-time
passcode sent to their registered phone number or email.
