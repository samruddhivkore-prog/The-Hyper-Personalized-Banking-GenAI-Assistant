# Security and Fraud Policy

Aisling Bank customers are not held liable for an unauthorised transaction reported within 60 days of the statement date it first appeared on, provided the customer did not share their login details or PIN with the person responsible. Suspected fraud should be reported immediately through the "Report a Problem" option in the mobile app or by calling the number on the back of the card. A card reported as compromised is deactivated within minutes, and a replacement is issued within 5-7 business days, or 1-2 business days for expedited shipping for a €15 fee. A login from a new device requires a one-time passcode sent by SMS or email, in addition to the password; biometric login by fingerprint or face recognition can also be enabled in the mobile app. Customers can set free real-time alerts for any transaction above a threshold they choose, as low as €1.00. After 5 consecutive failed login attempts, the account is locked for 30 minutes, or until the customer verifies their identity with a one-time passcode.
