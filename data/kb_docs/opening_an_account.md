# Opening an Account

To open an Aisling Bank account, an applicant must be at least 18 years old, be an Irish or EU resident, and provide a valid photo ID and proof of address dated within the last 3 months. Aisling Bank offers a current account for everyday spending and a High-Yield Savings account for growing a balance; a customer can hold both at the same time. There is no fee to open an account, and a current account has no monthly fee when it receives at least one salary or income deposit per month, or otherwise a €12.00 monthly fee applies, as set out in the General Account Fees policy. A new account is typically approved and ready to use within 1 business day of a complete application, and a debit card arrives by post within 5-7 business days.

How to open an account in the app.
1. Open the Aisling Bank app and tap Open an Account.
2. Enter your personal details and choose current account, High-Yield Savings, or both.
3. Upload your photo ID and proof of address.
4. Submit your application; most accounts are approved within 1 business day.
