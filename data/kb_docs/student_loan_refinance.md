# Student Loan Refinance

Student Loan Refinance lets eligible customers combine existing federal or private
student loans into a single new loan, potentially at a lower interest rate.

## Eligibility requirements
Applicants must be at least 18 years old, have graduated from an accredited
institution, and have a minimum of $5,000 in student loan debt to refinance. A credit
check is required, and applicants generally need a credit score of 660 or higher, or
a qualified co-signer.

## Interest rates
Fixed rates range from 4.5% to 9.99% APR. Variable rates range from 3.9% to 8.99%
APR, adjusted quarterly based on the prevailing benchmark rate. Rates depend on
credit profile, loan term, and whether a co-signer is used.

## Loan terms
Repayment terms of 5, 7, 10, 15, or 20 years are available. There is no prepayment
penalty for paying the loan off early.

## Important tradeoff: federal benefits
Refinancing federal student loans into a private loan through this program removes
access to federal benefits such as income-driven repayment plans, federal loan
forgiveness programs, and federal deferment options. Customers refinancing federal
loans should confirm they no longer need these protections before proceeding.

## Fees
There is no application fee, no origination fee, and no prepayment penalty.
