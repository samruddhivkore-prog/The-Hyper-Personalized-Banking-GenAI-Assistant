# Student Loan Refinance

Student Loan Refinance is only for combining existing student loans a customer already has into a single new loan, potentially at a lower interest rate; it is not for borrowing new money for tuition or living costs. A customer who is still studying and needs a new loan for tuition or living costs should see the separate Student Loan policy instead. To qualify, an applicant must be at least 18 years old, have graduated from an accredited institution, have a minimum of €5,000 in student loan debt to refinance, and have a credit score of 660 or higher, or a qualified co-signer. Fixed rates range from 4.5% to 9.99% APR; variable rates range from 3.9% to 8.99% APR and adjust quarterly with the prevailing benchmark rate. Repayment terms of 5, 7, 10, 15, or 20 years are available, with no application fee, no origination fee, and no penalty for paying the loan off early. Refinancing an existing government-backed student loan into this private loan removes access to any government repayment supports tied to the original loan, so customers should confirm they no longer need those supports before proceeding.
