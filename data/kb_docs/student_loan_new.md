# Student Loan

An Aisling Bank Student Loan is a new loan for paying university or college tuition fees and living costs while studying; it is only for new borrowing, not for combining or replacing existing student loans. (Customers who want to combine existing student loans into one new loan should see the separate Student Loan Refinance policy instead.) Loan amounts range from €2,000 to €40,000 per academic year, with a fixed rate of 5.5% to 8.5% APR set at approval based on course length and a qualified co-signer if required. Repayments do not begin until 6 months after the student graduates or leaves the course, after which the loan is repaid over 5 to 15 years. There is no application fee and no origination fee, and there is no penalty for repaying early. To be eligible, an applicant must be enrolled or accepted on a course at an accredited institution, be at least 18 years old, and provide proof of enrolment; applicants without an independent income history generally need a qualified co-signer. A decision is typically given within 5 business days of a complete application.
