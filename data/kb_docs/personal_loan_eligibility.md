# Personal Loan Eligibility

Personal loans are unsecured loans that can be used for debt consolidation, home
improvement, or major purchases, with fixed monthly payments.

## Eligibility requirements
Applicants must have held an account with the bank for at least 12 months, have an
income bracket of at least $25,000-$50,000, and have no existing personal loan open
with the bank. A credit check is required and the applicant's credit score must be at
least 620.

## Loan amounts and terms
Personal loans range from $1,000 to $50,000, with repayment terms of 12, 24, 36, 48,
or 60 months. There is no prepayment penalty for paying off the loan early.

## Interest rates
Fixed Annual Percentage Rates (APR) range from 7.99% to 21.99%, determined by credit
score, income, and loan term at the time of approval. Rates are locked for the full
term of the loan and do not change.

## Fees
There is a one-time origination fee of 1% to 5% of the loan amount, deducted from the
disbursed funds. There is no application fee and no annual fee.

## How long does approval take?
Most applications receive a decision within 1 business day. Funds are typically
disbursed to the applicant's checking account within 2 business days of approval.
