# Personal Loan

An Aisling Bank Personal Loan is an unsecured loan for buying a car, home improvement, debt consolidation, or other major purchases that are not property. Loan amounts range from €1,000 to €50,000, with repayment terms of 12, 24, 36, 48, or 60 months. Fixed Annual Percentage Rates range from 7.99% to 21.99%, set by credit score, income, and loan term at approval and locked for the life of the loan. A one-time origination fee of 1% to 5% of the loan amount is deducted from the funds disbursed; there is no application fee and no annual fee, and there is no penalty for paying the loan off early. To be eligible, an applicant must have held an Aisling Bank current account for at least 12 months, have an income of at least €25,000 per year, have no existing Personal Loan with Aisling Bank, and have a credit score of at least 620. Most applications receive a decision within 1 business day, and approved funds are typically paid into the applicant's current account within 2 business days. A Personal Loan cannot be used to buy or build a property; customers buying a home should apply for a Home Loan instead.
