# Account Tenure and Loyalty Benefits

Aisling Bank offers a small set of benefits that scale with how long a customer has held an account. At 6 months, a customer becomes eligible for High-Yield Savings. At 12 months, a customer becomes eligible for a Personal Loan, subject to credit and income requirements. At 24 months, a customer receives a one-time €50 loyalty credit automatically, with no action required. A customer whose account has been open for 5 years or more has the paper statement fee waived automatically, regardless of statement preference, and receives one free outgoing domestic wire transfer per calendar year. Tenure is measured in full months from the account opening date, not from when any individual product was added, and closing and reopening an account resets tenure to zero. A customer can view their exact tenure under Account Details in the mobile app.
