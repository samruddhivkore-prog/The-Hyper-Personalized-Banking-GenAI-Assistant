# Account Tenure and Loyalty Benefits

The bank offers a small set of benefits that scale with how long a customer has held
an account, in addition to product-specific eligibility rules described elsewhere.

## Tenure milestones
At 6 months, customers become eligible for High-Yield Savings. At 12 months,
customers become eligible for a Personal Loan (subject to credit and income
requirements). At 24 months, customers are eligible for a one-time $50 loyalty
statement credit, applied automatically with no action required.

## Fee waivers for long-tenured customers
Customers with an account open for 5 years or more automatically have the monthly
paper statement fee waived, regardless of statement preference, and receive one free
outgoing domestic wire transfer per calendar year.

## How tenure is calculated
Tenure is measured in full months from the account opening date, not the date any
specific product was added. Closing and reopening an account resets tenure to zero.

## Does tenure affect interest rates or loan terms?
Tenure affects eligibility (whether a product can be opened at all) but does not
directly change the interest rate or APR offered on loans or credit cards; those are
determined by credit profile and income at the time of application.

## Checking your tenure
Customers can view their exact account tenure in months under Settings > Account
Details in the mobile app or online banking.
