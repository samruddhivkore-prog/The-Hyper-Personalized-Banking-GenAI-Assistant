# ACH Transfer Policy

ACH (Automated Clearing House) transfers are the standard, low-cost way to move money
between U.S. bank accounts, including payroll deposits, bill payments, and
person-to-person transfers.

## Cost
Standard ACH transfers are free for personal checking and savings accounts. There is
no fee to send or receive a standard ACH transfer.

## Speed
Standard ACH transfers typically settle within 1-3 business days. Same-day ACH is
available for an additional fee of $5.00 per transfer, and settles by the end of the
same business day if submitted before 2:00 PM local time.

## Transfer limits
Personal accounts may send up to $10,000 per day and $25,000 per rolling 7-day period
via ACH transfer. Limits can be temporarily increased by contacting support and
completing an identity verification step.

## Failed or returned transfers
If an ACH transfer is returned (for example, due to insufficient funds in the
recipient's account or an invalid account number), the funds are automatically
returned to the sender's account within 1-2 business days, and a $0 fee applies for
personal accounts.

## When to use ACH instead of Real-Time Payments or wire
ACH is the best default choice for transfers that are not time-sensitive, since it is
free and handles most everyday transfer amounts. Choose Real-Time Payments when the
recipient needs funds within seconds, or a wire transfer for same-day delivery of
large amounts above $5,000.
