# Mobile Banking App

The Aisling Bank app is available for iOS and Android and covers most account needs without a branch visit. In the app, a customer can view balances, send a SEPA Credit Transfer, a Real-Time Payment, or initiate a wire, use Mobile Cheque Deposit, pay bills, freeze a card, view statements, and open a High-Yield Savings account or a Rewards Credit Card. The app is free to download and use; the customer's own mobile data charges may still apply. A customer can turn on Face ID, fingerprint, or PIN login instead of a password after their first successful password login on a device. A card can be frozen and unfrozen instantly in the app, which blocks new transactions without cancelling the card. The app needs an internet connection for every account action; a cached balance may show briefly while offline but is not guaranteed current. In-app chat support is available from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM, 7 days a week; outside those hours a customer can leave a message for a callback.
