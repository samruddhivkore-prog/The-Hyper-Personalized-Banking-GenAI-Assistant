# Mobile Banking App FAQ

The mobile banking app is available for iOS and Android and provides access to most
account features without visiting a branch.

## What can I do in the app?
The app supports checking balances, transferring funds (ACH, Real-Time Payments, and
wire initiation), Mobile Check Deposit, paying bills, freezing a debit card, viewing
statements, and opening new products such as High-Yield Savings or a Rewards Credit
Card.

## Is there a fee to use the app?
The mobile app is free to download and use. Standard message and data rates from the
customer's mobile carrier may apply.

## Biometric login
Customers can enable Face ID, fingerprint, or PIN-based login instead of entering a
password each time. Biometric login can be turned on under Settings > Security after
the first successful password login on a device.

## Card controls
Customers can instantly freeze and unfreeze a debit or credit card from the app,
which blocks new transactions but does not cancel the card. This is useful if a card
is temporarily misplaced.

## Offline access
The app requires an internet connection for all account actions. Cached balance
information may be displayed briefly while offline but is not guaranteed to be
current.

## Support within the app
Customers can chat with support directly in the app during business hours (8:00 AM to
9:00 PM local time, 7 days a week) or leave a message for a callback outside those
hours.
