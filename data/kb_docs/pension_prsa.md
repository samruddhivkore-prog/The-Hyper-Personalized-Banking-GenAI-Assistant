# Pension (PRSA)

Aisling Bank offers a Personal Retirement Savings Account, or PRSA, for customers saving for retirement outside of, or in addition to, a workplace pension. Contributions qualify for income tax relief up to age-related limits set by Revenue, and the relief percentage rises with the customer's age, from 15% of net relevant earnings under age 30 up to 40% from age 60 onward. There is no account opening fee and no annual maintenance fee; an annual fund management charge of up to 1% of the fund value applies to the underlying investment and is disclosed at the time of investment selection. Withdrawals are not permitted before age 60 except in limited circumstances such as serious ill health. A PRSA is available to any customer with earned income for the tax year, and there is no minimum account tenure required to open one.
