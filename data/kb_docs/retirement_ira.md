# Retirement IRA

The bank offers both Traditional and Roth Individual Retirement Accounts (IRAs) for
customers saving for retirement outside of an employer-sponsored plan.

## Contribution limits
For the current tax year, customers may contribute up to $7,000 per year, or $8,000
per year for customers age 50 or older, across all IRAs combined (Traditional and
Roth). These limits are set by federal regulation and may change annually.

## Traditional vs. Roth
Traditional IRA contributions may be tax-deductible depending on income, and
withdrawals in retirement are taxed as ordinary income. Roth IRA contributions are
made with after-tax dollars, and qualified withdrawals in retirement are tax-free.
Income limits apply to Roth IRA eligibility.

## Fees
There is no account opening fee and no annual maintenance fee for either IRA type.
Investment options held within the IRA may carry their own expense ratios, disclosed
separately at the time of investment selection.

## Early withdrawal
Withdrawals from a Traditional IRA before age 59½ are generally subject to a 10%
early withdrawal penalty in addition to ordinary income tax, with limited exceptions
such as a first-time home purchase up to $10,000. Roth IRA contributions (but not
earnings) can generally be withdrawn at any time without penalty.

## Eligibility
Available to any customer with earned income for the tax year. There is no minimum
account tenure required to open an IRA.
