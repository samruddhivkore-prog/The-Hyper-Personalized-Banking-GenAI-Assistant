# Rewards Credit Card

The Rewards Credit Card earns cash-back on everyday purchases with no annual fee for
the first year.

## Rewards structure
Cardholders earn 3% cash back on groceries and dining, 2% cash back on transit and
transfers, and 1% cash back on all other purchases. Cash back has no expiration and
can be redeemed as a statement credit or direct deposit in increments of $25.

## Annual fee
There is no annual fee for the first 12 months. After the first year, an annual fee
of $50 applies unless the cardholder spends at least $10,000 in the prior 12 months,
in which case the fee is waived.

## Eligibility
Applicants must have an active checking or savings account, an income bracket of at
least $25,000-$50,000, and no more than one Rewards Credit Card already open. A soft
credit check is used for pre-qualification; a hard credit check is required for final
approval.

## Interest rate
The standard variable Annual Percentage Rate (APR) ranges from 18.99% to 27.99%
depending on creditworthiness, determined at approval. A 0% introductory APR applies
to purchases for the first 6 months for newly approved accounts.

## Foreign transaction fees
There are no foreign transaction fees on purchases made outside the United States,
making this card a reasonable option for customers who travel internationally.
