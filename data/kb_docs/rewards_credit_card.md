# Rewards Credit Card

The Aisling Bank Rewards Credit Card earns cash back on everyday purchases, with no annual fee for the first 12 months. Cardholders earn 3% cash back on groceries and dining, 2% cash back on public transport purchases, and 1% cash back on everything else; cash back never expires and can be redeemed as a statement credit in increments of €25. After the first year, an annual fee of €50 applies unless the cardholder spent at least €10,000 in the prior 12 months, in which case the fee is waived. The standard variable Annual Percentage Rate ranges from 18.99% to 27.99% depending on creditworthiness at approval, with a 0% introductory rate on purchases for the first 6 months. To qualify, an applicant needs an active current or savings account, an income of at least €25,000 per year, and no more than one Rewards Credit Card already open. There are no foreign transaction fees on purchases made outside the European Union.
